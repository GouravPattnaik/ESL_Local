Branch Operations and Service Policy
=====================================
The Main Branch (B-MAIN) is the headquarters and handles high-net-worth individuals and corporate accounts.
Customers registered at B-MAIN receive priority support and access to a dedicated relationship manager.
The North Branch (B-NORTH), South Branch (B-SOUTH), and East Branch (B-EAST) serve retail customers.
All branches are authorized to open Savings and Current accounts.
Loan applications above 10,00,000 INR must be reviewed and approved by the Main Branch.
Each branch maintains its own daily transaction ledger which is reconciled centrally at end-of-day.
Inter-branch transfers are processed using the NEFT system within 2 business hours.
