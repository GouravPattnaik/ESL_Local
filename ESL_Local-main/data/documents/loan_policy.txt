Loan Eligibility and Risk Policy
=================================
Home Loans are offered to customers with a minimum credit score of 680 and a maximum loan-to-value (LTV) ratio of 80%.
Personal Loans are unsecured and require a minimum credit score of 650. Maximum tenure is 5 years.
Auto Loans require a minimum 20% down payment and have a maximum tenure of 7 years.
Any loan account that has missed 3 consecutive EMI payments is classified as Overdue and reported to the credit bureau.
Overdue loan accounts are flagged for manual review by the risk management team within 24 hours.
Loan interest rates are reviewed quarterly by the credit committee and are linked to the RBI repo rate.
Early repayment is permitted without penalty for Home Loans but attracts a 2% prepayment charge for Personal and Auto Loans.
